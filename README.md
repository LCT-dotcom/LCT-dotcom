# About Me

I am **Lý Chính Tân**, a fourth-year Biomedical Engineering student at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), VNU-HCM. My interests include biomedical signal processing, ECG and wearable PPG analysis, temporal machine learning and medical imaging.

I work on research implementations with clear evaluation protocols, documented limitations and reproducible experiments. I am also learning STM32 to understand microcontroller architecture and hardware structure more deeply, alongside Raspberry Pi and reading component datasheets.
